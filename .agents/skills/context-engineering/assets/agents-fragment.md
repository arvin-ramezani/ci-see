## Documentation and context

Use [documentation index](docs/index.md) to locate canonical knowledge relevant
to the task. Follow its cues instead of preloading all documents.

Use `$context-engineering` when establishing, auditing, or restructuring docs,
and when an edited document exceeds 150 lines. Review and execute a justified split
within authorized scope. If the skill is unavailable, apply the repository's
[context policy](docs/context-policy.md).

Update canonical documentation in the same change when behavior, architecture,
requirements, or operational contracts change.
Preserve requirements, conditions, failure cases, decisions, and stable IDs during
restructuring. Record original-to-destination mappings and verify relevant links.
Document why a cohesive file remains over the review threshold.
